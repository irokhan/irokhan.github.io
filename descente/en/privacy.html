Privacy policy

26 November 2026 · Descente for iPhone and iPad

Your saved game

Descente saves your progress, relics, outfit and preferences on your device so you can return to your run. You can play without creating an account. Deleting the app and its data in your device settings removes the local save.

Gameplay statistics

Irokhan uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand where players run into difficulty. The game sends it events such as your first grapple transfer, the depth you reach, falls, route choices and arrival at the lake. This helps the studio adjust the controls and difficulty.

The service also measures app opens and session length. It may associate events with an app installation identifier, technical details such as device model and game version, and an approximate location inferred from the IP address. It may record an event when an in-app purchase is completed.

Google automatically deletes individual analytics records after the retention period configured for the Analytics property. Aggregate statistical reports have separate retention rules. Google Analytics data retention.

The cape and tips

Apple processes payments. StoreKit gives the game the product and transaction status it needs to unlock or restore the Wind Cape or confirm a tip. Your payment details stay with Apple, which keeps its own purchase records. Apple’s privacy policy.

This website and support requests

GitHub Pages hosts these pages and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. If you email the studio, it receives your email address and the information you choose to send so it can respond. GitHub’s privacy statement.

Your choices and rights

Irokhan (Johan Pagès) publishes Descente. To request access to your data, ask for a correction or deletion, or object to processing where that right applies, email irokhan42@gmail.com and mention Descente. You can also contact your local data protection authority, such as the CNIL in France.
