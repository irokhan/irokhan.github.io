Need help with Descente?

Contact the studio

Email irokhan42@gmail.com. If something went wrong, include your iPhone or iPad model, your game version and what you were doing just before it happened. A screenshot can help us find the spot.

Where is my Wind Cape?

From the title screen, open Characters, then Outfits. Choose Wind cape and tap Equip outfit to wear it.

If you reinstalled the game or switched devices, sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Open the wardrobe and tap Restore purchases. You can then select and equip the cape.

How does tipping work?

Tap the Support heart on the title screen to see the price in your currency. Tap Tip to open Apple’s purchase confirmation. Each tip is a separate, optional payment. You can play the entire descent in your starting outfit.

How do I request a refund?

Sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase, choose Request a refund and select the item. Apple reviews the request. Request a refund from Apple.
